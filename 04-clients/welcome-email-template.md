# Client Welcome Email Template

**Status:** active
**Owner:** Sarah
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-01

## Purpose

The first thing a client reads after they sign. It has one job: turn a signature
into a working relationship where the client knows exactly what to send, where
to send it, and what they get back. Every question this email fails to answer
becomes an email later, and the whole point of a repeatable practice is that
month two costs less time than month one.

The website makes a specific promise on the client-upload section: *"Already a
client? Use the secure upload link from your welcome email."* This template is
what makes that promise true. There is no other place the client is given that
link.

## Trigger

SOP-002 step 8, immediately after the portal folder tree is created and the
per-client upload link exists. Send within one business day of the signed
engagement letter coming back. Never send it before the upload link is live,
because a welcome email that references a link the client cannot use is worse
than no welcome email.

## Before sending, these must be true

- Engagement letter is signed and archived, and the client has accepted the
  QuickBooks Payments recurring-payment enrollment from Intuit's email.
- The client's private upload link is created and tested by uploading a test
  file and deleting it.
- The client's folder tree exists.
- The first-close month and the first draft date are decided, not approximate.

## Fill these in before sending

| Placeholder | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `[CLIENT FIRST NAME]` | How they introduced themselves, not how the LLC is titled |
| `[BUSINESS NAME]` | The business, as they say it out loud |
| `[PLAN]` | Essentials, Standard, or Growing |
| `[MONTHLY PRICE]` | The flat monthly figure from the engagement letter |
| `[FIRST CLOSE MONTH]` | The first month we will close, e.g. "August 2026" |
| `[FIRST DELIVERY DATE]` | The 15th following that month, adjusted for weekends |
| `[FIRST DRAFT DATE]` | The date of the first ACH draft (QuickBooks Payments recurring) |
| `[UPLOAD LINK]` | This client's private upload link. Never a shared or public link |
| `[ACCOUNT LIST]` | The specific accounts we reconcile, named, e.g. "Chase business checking, Chase Ink card" |

## Rules this email follows and must keep following

- The word is **bookkeeping**, never accounting. Texas Occupations Code
  901.453(b). This applies to every client-facing document without exception.
- No em dashes. Plain words. If a sentence needs a comma or a period instead,
  use one.
- Nothing is promised here that is not already in the engagement letter. If the
  two ever disagree, the engagement letter governs and this template is wrong.
- No tax advice, no payroll, no bill paying, no moving the client's money. The
  email states the boundary rather than leaving the client to discover it.
- Statements and documents move through the upload link only, never as email
  attachments. Guardrail 7.

## The email

**Subject:** Welcome to The Relief Pros, [BUSINESS NAME]. Here is everything you need.

**Body:**

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

Welcome aboard. Everything is set up on our end, and this email has the three
things you need: where to send documents, what we need each month, and what you
get back.

**Your secure upload link**

[UPLOAD LINK]

That link is yours alone. Bookmark it. Anything you would normally email us goes
there instead: statements, receipts, a photo of a bill, anything. It is
encrypted and it is the only place client documents live, which is why we ask
you not to send financial documents as email attachments. Email is not a safe
place for your bank statements, and we would rather be slightly inconvenient
than casual about it.

**What we need from you each month**

Between the 1st and the 5th, upload these for the month that just ended:

1. Statements for every account we reconcile: [ACCOUNT LIST]
2. Any loan or line of credit statements
3. Receipts or invoices for anything unusual, large, or hard to identify from
   the bank description
4. A one-line note about anything out of the ordinary, for example an owner
   draw, a new piece of equipment, or a personal expense that hit the business
   card by accident

You will get an automatic reminder on the 1st, the 3rd, and the 5th. Ignore
them once everything is in.

If something is missing, we do not stop and wait. We close on time, park the
affected item, and send a short list with your reports of what was parked and
why.

**What you get back**

By the [FIRST DELIVERY DATE] for [FIRST CLOSE MONTH], and by the 15th every
month after that:

- Profit and loss statement
- Balance sheet

Every account is reconciled to the actual statement, to the penny, before
anything is delivered. If a number does not tie to a real document, it does not
go out.

**Your plan and billing**

- Plan: [PLAN], $[MONTHLY PRICE] per month, flat
- Payment setup arrives in a separate email from Intuit (QuickBooks). You
  enter your bank details directly with Intuit and accept the draft there. We
  never see or store your bank numbers.
- First draft: [FIRST DRAFT DATE], then the 1st of each month
- A receipt is emailed automatically after every draft
- Month to month. Thirty days' notice, no penalty, and we send you your files

**What we do not do, so there are no surprises**

We do bookkeeping only. We do not prepare or file tax returns, we do not run
payroll, and we do not pay your bills or move money in or out of your accounts.
That last one is deliberate. Keeping payment authority with you is the correct
control, and it is not something we make exceptions on.

Your books are built to hand straight to your CPA. If you do not have one, we
are happy to point you toward a few good ones. We take nothing for that
referral, so the recommendation is worth something.

**Reaching us**

Just reply to this email. It comes to a person, not a ticket queue, and you get
an answer within one business day. If it is faster to say out loud, call
469-702-1185, Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.

One favor: if anything in this email is unclear, tell me now rather than working
around it. The first month is where habits get set, and a two-minute question
today saves us both a mess in October.

Glad to have you.

Sarah Bowen
The Relief Pros, LLC

## Variant: client who is starting with a cleanup

Insert this block after the "Your secure upload link" section, and change the
"What you get back" dates to the cleanup delivery date.

> **First, the cleanup**
>
> Before monthly service starts, we are getting [FIRST CLOSE MONTH] and
> everything behind it current. Here is the shape of that:
>
> - We need statements for every month in the cleanup period, uploaded to the
>   link above. That is the one thing that determines how fast this goes.
> - The price is [CLEANUP PRICE], flat, exactly as quoted. It does not change
>   once we start.
> - Expect roughly [CLEANUP WEEKS] weeks. If it will be longer, you hear it from
>   us before the date, not after.
> - We will come back to you with questions in one batch rather than a trickle
>   of one-off emails.
>
> When the cleanup is delivered, monthly service begins with [FIRST CLOSE
> MONTH], and your first month of monthly bookkeeping is included as part of the
> bundle.

## Variant: the three-day nudge, if nothing has been uploaded

Send only once, three business days after the welcome email, and only if the
upload folder is still empty.

**Subject:** Quick one, [CLIENT FIRST NAME]

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

No rush, just closing the loop. Nothing has landed in your upload folder yet, so
I want to make sure the link is working on your end rather than assume it is.

[UPLOAD LINK]

If it opens and you are simply gathering statements, ignore this. If it does not
open, reply and I will send a fresh one today.

Sarah

## After sending

1. Log the send date on the client's onboarding checklist (SOP-002 step 10).
2. Confirm the first upload lands. An empty folder on day 3 triggers the nudge
   above; an empty folder on day 5 moves the client to the document chase list
   (SOP-004).
3. The filled-in copy of this email contains client data. It stays in the
   client's archive. It never goes in this repo, in Drive, or in the Claude
   Project.
