If your business is young, read this part

Three things new Texas LLC owners find out late.

  • Texas still wants an annual filing, even with no tax due. Owners look for an annual report through the Secretary of State, do not find one, and assume there is nothing. The Public Information Report is due May 15 regardless of whether you owe franchise tax. We wrote up what it is and what happens when it slips, including the part about liability protection that nobody mentions.
  • Mixing personal and business spending is cheap to prevent and expensive to untangle. One card for the business, from day one. Undoing two years of mixed transactions is one of the more tedious cleanups there is.
  • W-9s are easy to collect before you pay someone and nearly impossible after. If you use subcontractors, get the form up front. Every January, somebody is chasing a sub who has stopped answering the phone.

None of that is complicated. It is just unfamiliar, and unfamiliar things get postponed. Books that start clean stay cheap to maintain, which is the entire argument for setting this up in year one instead of year three.
