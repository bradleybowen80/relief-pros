Questions, answered straight

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between you and my CPA or tax preparer?

We keep your books accurate all year. Your CPA uses those books to file your taxes. We don't prepare taxes or give tax advice, and that's on purpose: it means your CPA and we each do what we're best at, and tax time gets easy for everyone.

My books are months (or years) behind. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup is one of our specialties. We'll quote a one-time cleanup project after your Free Books Checkup, get you current, and then keep you current monthly.

Do you only work with businesses near Frisco?

No. We're based in Frisco and work 100% remotely with small businesses all across Texas. Documents move through a secure portal and meetings happen on Zoom, on a schedule that fits you.

I already have a bookkeeper. Is switching a pain?

No. We handle the transition: we review your current QuickBooks file, note any issues, and pick up cleanly. Most switches take under two weeks.

What affects my price?

Three things: how many bank and credit card accounts you have, how many transactions run through them, and whether you need extras like 1099 tracking. That's why our tiers are listed by exactly those numbers, and your quote is confirmed in your free checkup. Flat monthly fee, no surprises.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. We work in your QuickBooks Online file through secure access you grant and can revoke anytime, documents move through an encrypted portal instead of email, and we never store your banking credentials. We also never hold authority to move your money. Ever.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. Your books and your QuickBooks file are always yours.

Do you pay my bills or run my payroll?

No, and you want it that way. We record and reconcile everything, but you keep full control of your money. We coordinate with your payroll provider so it all lands correctly in the books.
