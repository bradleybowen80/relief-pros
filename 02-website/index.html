Questions owners actually ask

Straight answers, before you ever call.

What's the difference between you and my CPA or tax preparer?

We keep your books accurate all year. Your CPA uses those books to file your taxes. We don't prepare taxes or give tax advice, on purpose: it means clean handoffs, no turf wars, and nobody here trying to upsell you a tax package.

My books are months (or years) behind. Can you help?

Yes, that's our specialty, and there's no judgment here. Cleanup is $1,000 flat for books up to 12 months behind, quoted in writing after your free Books Checkup. Further behind than that? We'll give you an honest number up front.

Do you only work with businesses near Frisco?

No. We're based in Frisco and work 100% remotely with businesses anywhere in Texas. Documents move through a secure portal, meetings happen on Zoom, and your books don't care about the miles.

I already have a bookkeeper. Is switching a pain?

No. We handle the transition: we review your current QuickBooks file, flag any issues, and pick up cleanly. Plan on under two weeks, and your old bookkeeper never has to take an awkward call from you.

What affects my price?

Two numbers: how many bank and credit card accounts you have, and how many transactions run through them. That's why the tiers above are listed by exactly those numbers. Your quote is confirmed in your free Checkup and locked in writing.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. You grant us access to your QuickBooks Online, which you can see and revoke anytime. Documents move through an encrypted portal instead of email, we never store your banking logins, and we never hold authority to move your money. Ever.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel with 30 days' notice. Your books and your QuickBooks file are always yours, fully up to date on the day you go.

Do you pay my bills or run my payroll?

No, and you want it that way. We record and reconcile everything, but you keep full control of your money. We coordinate with your payroll provider so it all lands correctly in the books.

I'm not on QuickBooks Online yet. Can you still help?

QuickBooks Online is the only tool we work in, on purpose. If you're on spreadsheets or QuickBooks Desktop, your free Books Checkup will tell you honestly whether moving makes sense and what it would involve. No pressure either way.

How fast can you start?

We reply within one business day, checkups finish inside a week, and you're fully onboarded in about two weeks. The one bottleneck: cleanup projects are capped at three per month, so if you're behind, the checkup is worth doing sooner than later.
