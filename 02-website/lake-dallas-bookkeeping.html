Different from the boomtowns

You have been doing this a while.

Up the road in Celina and Prosper, half the businesses are younger than the streets they sit on. Lake Dallas is not that. Here, the business has usually been open for years, the owner knows every customer, and the books have been done the same way since the beginning: at the kitchen table, on a Sunday, by the person who least wants to be doing it.

That is not a broken system. It worked. But there is a point where the hours it eats stop being worth it, and most owners hit that point long before they do anything about it, because switching feels like more trouble than continuing.

It is not. We review your file, note anything that needs attention, and pick up from where you are. Most handoffs take under two weeks, and you never have to explain to anyone why you are making a change.

One thing worth flagging if your business has been around a while: Texas requires an annual Public Information Report even in years when no franchise tax is owed, and missing it long enough has consequences that reach past a late fee. We explained what it is and why owners miss it, because a business that has been running for a decade is exactly the kind that assumes it is already handled.

Lake Dallas questions

Fair questions from people who have managed fine so far.

I have done my own books for years. Is it worth paying someone now?

Run the math on your own time. If the books take you six hours a month and you would rather spend those hours on the business or not working at all, $250 is a straightforward trade. If you genuinely do not mind doing them, keep doing them. We are not going to talk you into something you do not need.

My books are a mess and I am embarrassed about it.

Nobody here is judging, and honestly, you are describing most of the businesses that call us. Cleanup is a defined flat-price project, quoted in writing before anything starts. Once you are current, staying current is the easy part.

Do I have to be on QuickBooks Online?

Yes, that is the only tool we work in, on purpose. If you are on spreadsheets or QuickBooks Desktop, we will tell you honestly whether moving makes sense and what it involves. No pressure either way.
