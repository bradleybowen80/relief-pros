# 03-marketing

How clients find and choose the business: positioning, outreach copy, email
sequences, and channel experiments.

## What belongs here

- Positioning and messaging docs (who we serve, the offer, the promise)
- Cold email and outreach templates
- Channel notes: what was tried, what it cost, what it produced

## Rules that apply here

- All copy says "bookkeeping" (legal gate, see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)).
- Brad stays invisible: no founder story with his name or face. The business
  and Sarah are the public identity.
- Favor channels where something other than a salesperson brings the buyer:
  search, directories, referral loops, free tools.
