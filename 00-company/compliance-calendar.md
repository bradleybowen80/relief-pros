## How to use this calendar

1. Brad sets a reminder for each dated item at its lead time (for example, 60 days before a SAM.gov renewal, 30 days before June 30 recert).
2. Before acting on any dated item, re-verify the date and the current rule against the primary source. Platform dates in particular change.
3. When a platform migration completes or a rule changes, update this file and note the change.
