# 00-company

The company's foundation: what it is, what it may and may not do, and where
the off-repo documents live.

## What belongs here

- Formation and registration facts (LLC details, EIN notes, registered agent)
- The compliance calendar (franchise tax, registrations, renewals)
- The automation and backup scheme (infrastructure build list, backup layers)
- Toolchain notes (settled technical facts)
- [drive-registry.md](drive-registry.md), the single index of every Google
  Drive/Docs/Sheets document the business uses
- Insurance, banking, and vendor account notes (facts and pointers, never
  credentials)

## Index

- [compliance-calendar.md](compliance-calendar.md): every recurring deadline

- [drive-registry.md](drive-registry.md): where everything in Drive lives
- [drive-backups/](drive-backups/README.md): on-disk snapshots of Drive documents
