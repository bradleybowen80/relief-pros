# SOP-010: Client Screening Scorecard

**Status:** active
**Owner:** Sarah
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-28

## Purpose

Score every prospect before a proposal goes out, and re-score every client at renewal, so bad-fit clients never enter or linger. Bad-fit clients are the single largest capacity killer in the practitioner literature. A low score is a decline, and a decline is not a failure; it is the SOP working.

## Trigger

Before every proposal, and at each client's renewal.

## Hard filters (any one of these is an automatic decline)

- [ ] Client refuses ACH autodraft. **Decline.** ACH autodraft is the only payment model.
- [ ] Client refuses to use the document portal. **Decline.** Portal-only is a security rule.
- [ ] Client wants an excluded service as a condition (payroll, AP, AR, tax prep, or payment authority over their accounts). **Decline and refer.**

If any hard filter trips, stop here. Do not score. Do not send a proposal.

## Scorecard (score each 1 to 5, 5 is best)

| Factor | 5 | 1 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business age | 2+ years, stable | Brand new | Prefer 2+ years. New businesses are the industry's worst clients; value them only as a month-12-to-14 drip. |
| Vertical fit | Home-services contractor or professional services | Poor-fit or high-churn | Contractors are primary (40 to 50% of roster). Professional services are the ballast (25 to 35%). |
| Restaurant exposure | Not a restaurant | Restaurant when roster is already at cap | Restaurants opportunistic only, capped at 25% of roster. ~49% close within five years; that churn is unavoidable. |
| Books condition | Clean, ~5 hours/mo | Deep mess, cleanup required | Drives hours per client, the core economic variable. A mess is a separate cleanup engagement (SOP-003), quoted separately. |
| Document discipline | Sends documents promptly | Chronically late | Chronic lateness burns capacity even though it never delays a close. |
| Payment reliability (renewal) | Never a failed draft | Repeated failed drafts | At renewal only. Repeat SOP-005 offenders score low. |
| Scope behavior | Respects scope | Constant out-of-scope asks | Scope creep is the top fixed-fee failure mode. |

## Reading the score

- **Any hard filter tripped:** decline, regardless of score.
- **Low overall or a deep-mess books score:** either decline, or convert to a properly quoted cleanup (SOP-003) before considering monthly service.
- **Restaurant that would push the roster over 25%:** decline or defer until the roster has room.
- **Strong score, no hard filters:** proceed to the engagement pre-flight (SOP-009), then onboarding (SOP-002).

## Renewal use

1. Re-score each client at renewal using the full table, including payment reliability and document discipline.
2. A client who has become chronically late, a repeat failed-draft offender, or a scope-creep problem is a candidate for a price step, a scope reset, or non-renewal.

## Done when

- Every proposal was preceded by a completed scorecard with no tripped hard filters.
- Each renewal has a fresh score on file.
- Any decline or non-renewal decision is recorded with its reason.

## If something goes wrong

- **A prospect is eager and lucrative but trips a hard filter:** still a decline. The hard filters are not overridden by revenue. (For example, a restaurant over the cap, or a client refusing autodraft.)
- **A good client slid into chronic lateness:** address it at renewal with a price step or scope reset, not by absorbing the extra work or by having Sarah chase.
