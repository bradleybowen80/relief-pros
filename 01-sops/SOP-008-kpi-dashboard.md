# SOP-008: KPI Dashboard, Reviewed Monthly

**Status:** active
**Owner:** Sarah (reviews), Brad (builds and maintains the dashboard)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-28

## Purpose

Watch the small number of metrics that predict whether the firm is healthy, against the decision gates set before feelings are involved. Reviewed monthly so a problem shows up as a number, not a surprise.

## Trigger

Monthly, after all closes are delivered (after the 15th). Also read against the decision gates at day 90, month 6, month 12, month 18, and month 30 to 36.

## Metrics tracked

| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MRR and client count | Against the gate for the current month | The top-line health signal; compared to the decision gates below |
| Hours per client | 9 or below early, trending to 5 | The whole economic bet is small, clean, low-hour clients (Section 7 caveat) |
| Close-by-day | 100% of closes delivered by the 15th | Late closes are the first sign of overload |
| Failed drafts outstanding | Zero past day 10 | A receivable forming is a policy failure (SOP-005) |
| Pipeline conversations | 10 per month during growth | Acquisition is the real risk, not delivery |
| Google reviews | +1 per month minimum | Reviews are the local-SEO ranking lever and require clients |

## Decision gates (read monthly, act at the milestone)

| Gate | Threshold | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| Day 90 | 3 signed clients, from ~30 conversations and ~10 proposals | Diagnose the channel, not the effort. Pivot niche or to bookkeeping-firm subcontracting within 30 days |
| Month 6 | 3 to 5 clients, ~$1,500 to $2,500 MRR | Yellow. Secure an overflow subcontract as an income bridge and for supervised reps |
| Month 12 | 8 to 12 clients, ~$4,000 to $6,000 MRR | Red. Begin tuck-in and employment conversations while the book still has sale value |
| Month 18 | Net income within reach of the ~$72,000 full-charge floor | Execute exit: sell or merge the book, take employment |
| Month 30 to 36 | ~$15,000 to $20,000 MRR trajectory | Below this the firm loses to the corporate path on money alone. Continue only for autonomy, household fit, and the sellable asset, and say so out loud |

Every gate exits into the DFW salary table, not into zero. The worst realistic case is roughly 18 months of forgone salary minus the book's sale value, ending with Sarah more employable than at launch.

## Steps

1. After the 15th, pull the month's numbers for each metric above.
2. Compare each to its target. Flag every miss.
3. At a milestone month, compare to the decision gate and record the verdict (green/yellow/red) and the action the gate prescribes if missed.
4. Log the month's dashboard and note any action taken.

## Done when

- Every metric is recorded for the month with a pass/fail against target.
- At a milestone month, the gate verdict and prescribed action are recorded.
- Any red gate has a dated decision attached, not a deferral.

## If something goes wrong

- **Hours per client keep rising past 9:** this is the capacity killer. Diagnose the client mix (too many complex or messy clients) before adding any new client.
- **Closes slip past the 15th:** stop taking new clients until the roster closes on time again. Capacity, not effort, is the constraint.
- **A gate is missed:** follow the gate's prescribed action on schedule. The gates exist to force the decision before it becomes emotional. Do not move the goalposts.
