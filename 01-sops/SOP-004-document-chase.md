# SOP-004: Document Chase

**Status:** active
**Owner:** Brad (Sarah never chases documents)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-01

## Purpose

Get each client's statements, receipts, and supporting documents in on time, through automation first and Brad second, so the close (SOP-001) starts on schedule. Sarah's production time is never spent chasing paper. Missing documents never delay a close; they are parked and disclosed instead.

## Trigger

The 1st of each month, when the portal begins auto-requesting the prior month's documents. Runs through roughly the 5th, then Brad handles stragglers.

## Steps

### 1. Automated requests (days 1, 3, 5)

1. The portal auto-requests the month's documents on the 1st.
2. Automated reminders send on day 1, day 3, and day 5 to any client with outstanding items.
3. Reminders are polite, specific (name the exact missing document), and routed through the portal.

### 2. Brad chases stragglers (after day 5)

1. On day 5, pull the list of clients with still-missing documents.
2. Brad personally follows up with each. This is administrative contact only, not accounting discussion. Any accounting question the client raises goes to Sarah's queue.
3. Log each contact and the client's response.

### 3. Hand off to the close

1. By the close start (target the 10th), hand the close the complete document set per client, plus a list of anything still missing.
2. Missing items are parked in a labeled holding account and disclosed in the parked-items schedule (SOP-001 steps 9 and 10). The close proceeds on time regardless.

### 4. Flag chronic offenders

1. A client who is repeatedly late is logged for the renewal scorecard (SOP-010).
2. Chronic lateness is a screening and renewal issue, handled at renewal, not by delaying closes or by Sarah chasing harder.

## Done when

- Automated reminders fired on days 1, 3, and 5 to every client with outstanding items.
- Brad has personally followed up with every straggler after day 5.
- The close received a complete document set plus a named list of anything missing.
- Chronic offenders are logged for renewal review.

## If something goes wrong

- **A client never sends a document:** do not delay the close. Park the affected amount and disclose it (SOP-001 step 9). Firm policy, stated in the engagement letter.
- **A client raises an accounting question during a chase:** route it to Sarah. Brad does not answer accounting questions. (Separation of duties, guardrail 5.)
- **A client is chronically late every month:** log for the renewal scorecard. Do not absorb it as extra effort indefinitely.
