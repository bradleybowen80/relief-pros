## Rules that apply here

- **Verify before writing.** QuickBooks Online changes constantly and most
  third-party material is stale. Every menu path, shortcut, and figure gets
  checked against Intuit's own help articles, and anything that cannot be
  verified is labelled as unverified rather than stated as fact.
- **Date every document.** A training guide with no review date is a trap.
- When a path in here turns out to be wrong in a live file, fix it here rather
  than working around it, and move the review date.
- The HTML is generated from the markdown. Edit the markdown, then regenerate.
